Frequently asked questions

Do we need AI or is automation enough?

Ordinary automation is often enough. AI is needed when text must be read and understood.

What does an AI solution cost?

It depends on task and volume. After a small test you get a proposal with price and running cost.

How is our data protected?

Data is handled under GDPR. Sensitive data can be processed by a model on your own server.

Does AI replace staff?

The goal is to remove boring work, so staff can spend their time on customers and decisions.
