Services

AI with a person still in charge

AI is good at suggesting. Important decisions are made by a person.

The best AI solutions for small businesses do the groundwork and leave the decision. That makes them safer and easier to trust. I build in approvals, sampling and logging from the start, so you can always see what the AI did and why.

Building blocks

  • Approval before anything is sent to customers
  • Samples reviewed regularly
  • A log of every AI judgement
  • Alerts only when a person is really needed

Why

  • Errors are caught early and fixed in the instructions
  • Staff keep control of customer contact
  • Following GDPR and internal rules gets easier

Frequently asked questions

Do approvals not slow things down?

A little, but the AI has already done the groundwork. Approval takes seconds instead of minutes.

Can approval be removed later?

For tasks where the AI has proven reliable, sampling can replace per-case approval. You decide.
