Services

AI that sorts the inbox

The right case to the right person, with a summary and a suggested reply.

A shared inbox fills with enquiries, questions, invoices and advertising. AI can read each email, decide what it is about, how urgent it is and who should take it. A suggested reply can be written, but a person sends it.

What AI can do

  • Sort email by type and urgency
  • Summarise long threads
  • Suggest replies based on your earlier replies and facts
  • Alert when something really needs a person

Limits

  • AI does not send replies to customers without a person approving
  • Personal data is handled under GDPR
  • Every judgement is logged so it can be reviewed

Frequently asked questions

Are our emails sent to an external AI service?

It depends on the solution. An AI model can also run on your own server, so the emails stay with you.

How do we know the AI is right?

A sample is reviewed regularly, and wrong judgements are used to improve the instructions.
